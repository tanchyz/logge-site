How do I log an event?

Tap [+] on the Log screen, pick a tracker, and you’re done. Long-press trackers to log several at once, or change the date to backdate an event.

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. Use Settings › Export Data to save a PDF, CSV or JSON copy. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Restoring Logge Pro

Open any Pro feature and tap “Restore Purchase”. Subscriptions are managed in iOS Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

Cancelling a subscription

Go to iOS Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions › Logge Pro › Cancel Subscription. Pro features stay active until the end of the current period.
